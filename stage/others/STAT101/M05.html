Discrete Random VariableContinuous Random Variable
$$\sum P(x) = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad 0 \le P(x) \le 1$$
$$\mu = \sum [x \cdot P(x)], \quad \sigma = \sqrt{\sum [x^2 \cdot P(x)] - \mu^2}$$
$$\text{Max usual} = \mu + 2\sigma, \quad \text{Min usual} = \mu - 2\sigma$$
$$q = 1 - p$$
$$P(x) = \frac{n!}{(n - x)!x!} \cdot p^x \cdot q^{n-x}$$
$$\mu = np, \quad \sigma^2 = npq, \quad \sigma = \sqrt{npq}$$
