أهداف التعلم

text 
Example:
Virtual address space = 8K (2^13 bytes) -> 13 bits total
Physical address space = 4K (2^12 bytes) -> 12 bits total
Page size = 1024 bytes (2^10) -> Offset is 10 bits

Virtual Address Breakdown:
- Page Field: 13 - 10 = 3 bits (8 virtual pages)
- Offset: 10 bits

Physical Address Breakdown:
- Frame Field: 12 - 10 = 2 bits (4 physical frames)
- Offset: 10 bits
$$EAT = (1 - p)(2 \times t_m) + p(t_d)$$
